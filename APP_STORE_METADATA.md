**Easy Flyer - Your Ultimate Savings Companion**

Never miss a great deal again! Easy Flyer brings you the best local deals, flyers, and promotional offers from your favorite stores, all in one convenient app.

**🎯 Key Features:**
• **Local Deals**: Discover amazing offers from stores in your area
• **QR Code Scanner**: Instantly scan QR codes on flyers to unlock exclusive deals
• **Smart Categories**: Browse deals by category - groceries, electronics, fashion, and more
• **Deal Sharing**: Share great finds with friends and family
• **Personalized Experience**: Get deals tailored to your location and preferences
• **Offline Access**: View saved deals even without internet connection

**💰 How It Works:**
1. Enter your postal code to see local deals
2. Browse categories or search for specific items
3. Tap on deals to view full details and terms
4. Scan QR codes directly from flyers
5. Share amazing deals with others
