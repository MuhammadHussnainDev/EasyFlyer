# Privacy Policy for Easy Flyer

**Last updated: [Date]**

## Introduction

Easy Flyer ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Postal Code**: We collect your postal code to show you relevant local deals and offers
- **User ID**: A unique identifier to associate your account with your preferences
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, and app version for technical support

### Usage Information
- **App Usage**: How you interact with the app, features used, and time spent
- **Deal Interactions**: Which deals you view, share, or interact with
- **Location Data**: General location based on postal code (not precise GPS coordinates)

### Camera and Photo Access
- **QR Code Scanning**: Camera access to scan QR codes on flyers
- **Photo Sharing**: Access to save and share flyer images

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide personalized deals and offers based on your location
- Improve our app functionality and user experience
- Send you relevant notifications about new deals
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance our services
- Provide customer support

## Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in these circumstances:

- **Service Providers**: With trusted partners who help us operate our app
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Business Transfers**: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information

## Children's Privacy
